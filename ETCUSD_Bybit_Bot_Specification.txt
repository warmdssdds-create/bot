SECTION 0: DISCLAIMER AND REALISTIC EXPECTATIONS
====================================================================

0.1 No trading system, human or automated, can guarantee a fixed win
rate such as 85% across all market conditions. Win rate is an output
of live and historical testing, not an input you can promise before
a strategy has been run. Any vendor or document claiming a guaranteed
85% win rate before backtesting is making an unverifiable claim.

0.2 This document instead gives you a complete, buildable framework:
a defined strategy, defined risk rules, a defined connection to
Bybit, and a defined backtesting process. You run the backtest and
forward test on your own historical and live data to discover the
actual win rate this strategy produces on ETCUSD, then you tune
parameters using the optimization process in Section 16.

0.3 High win-rate strategies (75-85%) do exist in practice, but they
share three properties: small average reward-to-risk ratios (meaning
many small wins offset occasional larger losses), strict regime
filtering (the bot refuses to trade in conditions it was not built
for), and continuous re-calibration. This document builds the bot
around those three properties rather than around a marketing number.

0.4 Cryptocurrency derivatives trading carries a high risk of capital
loss, including on ETCUSD perpetual contracts, which are more
volatile than most large-cap pairs. Leverage amplifies both gains
and losses. Nothing in this document is financial advice. Position
sizing, leverage, and go-live decisions are the operator's
responsibility.

0.5 The bot described here interacts only with Bybit's official,
publicly documented REST and WebSocket API endpoints using your own
API key and secret. It does not access any other user's account, it
does not exploit any vulnerability, and it does not perform any
action a human trader could not perform manually through the Bybit
web interface. It automates a specific, disclosed, rules-based
strategy.
